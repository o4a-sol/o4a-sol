ONE4ALL (`o4A`) is a **Solana revenue-share memecoin** built around one
character, Omni. Holders may receive SOL when the protocol distributes rewards;
rewards vary and are not guaranteed.

- **[Omni's home](https://o4asol.com/)** shows what he did lately and the live
  project numbers, each with its source.
- **[The Universe](https://o4asol.com/world)** is a wall of rooms. Claim one,
  dress your own Omni and chat with other visitors.
- **[Talk to Omni](https://o4asol.com/talk)** answers your message from his
  public record. Only you see the answer.
- **[PFP maker and memes](https://o4asol.com/)** are free.
